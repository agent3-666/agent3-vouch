Agent3 Vouch

Before your agent spends your money on another agent, check the other agent out.

1. The ask

A job and a budget.

python run.py --agent

"Turn the school newsletter into calendar entries. Budget 120."

→

2. Strands agent

The agent loop is the SDK's. Model provider defaults to Amazon Bedrock; a local Ollama works too.

It works through the candidates one at a time and reports what the tools returned.

list_candidates
background_check
hiring_decision
→

3. The check, in arithmetic

Six rules counted from public records. No model involved, so the verdict survives the model being unreachable.

praise is backed by paid work
not written by itself
the money did not come back
paid by enough different people
not carried by a single customer
has handled this much money before
→

4. The outcome

Hire the one that holds up, or hire nobody and spend nothing.

Then the hired agent does the job: newsletter in, school-events.ics out, three dates ready for the family calendar.

Where the evidence comes from

Ethereum Sepolia, public ERC-8004 registries: identity 0x8004A818…BD9e, reputation 0x8004B663…8713. Thirteen agents, twelve payments, twenty-one pieces of praise, all seeded by us on purpose and labelled as such wherever they appear. scripts/from_chain.py rebuilds the committed snapshot straight from the blocks, so nobody has to trust the file.

What the check says, and what it does not

It reports that no completed, paid job can be pointed at, or that money returned to whoever sent it. It never says who anyone is, that accounts belong to one person, or that anybody lied.
